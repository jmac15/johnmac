Introduction

Teaching in a Digital Age is an open textbook by Dr. Tony Bates offering guidelines for designing teaching and learning. This is the 2019-updated edition, recreated here from its HTML and PDF editions. It looks at the fundamental changes under way in education, the nature of knowledge, methods of teaching both on campus and online, MOOCs, the role of technology and media, modes of delivery, open education, quality assurance, and the support that teachers and instructors need.
